What is Coronavirus?

Corona viruses are a large family of viruses which may cause illness in animals or humans. In humans, several coronaviruses are known to cause respiratory infections ranging from the common cold to more severe diseases.

  • • Novel virus strain discovered
  • • Spreads via respiratory droplets
  • • Incubation period varies from 2-14 days

Why Is It Dangerous?

Because it affects the lungs severely and spreads incredibly fast before symptoms even start appearing, making tracking difficult.

  • • Highly contagious nature
  • • Severe respiratory distress
  • • Higher risk for elderly and chronic illnesses

How To Prevent Coronavirus?

You can protect yourself and help prevent spreading the virus to others if you complete simple safety measures closely.

  • Avoid close contact with sick people
  • Avoid touching eyes, nose, and mouth
  • Clean and disinfect frequently touched surfaces
